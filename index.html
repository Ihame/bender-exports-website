About Us

Bender Coffee Company Ltd was established in 2017 by Mr. Horanimpundu Jean Paul and Ms Uwamariya Odette, driven by their passion for coffee and their commitment to contributing to Rwanda’s development. Their vision was to create a company that not only values the richness of Rwandan coffee but also empowers rural farmers by ensuring they benefit directly from their hard work To achieve this, Bender Coffee invested in modern washing stations and secure warehouses that protect the quality of coffee from harvest to export. Beyond infrastructure, the company works hand-in-hand with farmers, offering practical training and guidance to ensure that every step—from planting to processing—meets the highest standards.
