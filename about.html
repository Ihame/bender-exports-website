Our Story

At Bender Coffee, our purpose is to honor Rwanda’s land and the farmers who bring it to life. We exist to transform harvests into exceptional coffee while strengthening the communities behind every bean. Our mission is to process and craft quality coffee across regions—Kirehe, Rusizi, and Kamonyi—ensuring each station reflects the unique character of its land. Our vision is to see Rwandan coffee celebrated worldwide, while farmers thrive locally through sustainable practices. We pledge to provide 1,000,000 coffee seedlings over the next three years, starting this year, and we are already distributing them. Alongside seedlings, we offer training and guidance, supporting farmers from the very beginning of the process through to harvest. By walking with them from seed to cup, we empower farmers to grow with confidence, preserve traditions, and share Rwanda’s finest flavors with the world.
